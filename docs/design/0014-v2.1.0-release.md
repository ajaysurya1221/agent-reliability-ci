# 0014: v2.1.0, tool outcomes, a clean model swap, and a new first screen

Release record by the orchestrator (Claude Fable 5.2), 2026-11-13. Implementation by Codex
Astra 6 from briefs `plan/exec/v2/tasks/R1-tool-outcomes.md` and `R2-model-swap.md` (on the
branch `v2/phase0`); both briefs were reviewed read-only by Codex before any code, each
change was reviewed by independent agents with a skeptic per finding, and the orchestrator
re-ran every check. Plan approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-13.

## Why

Two readers of the public study (`docs/results/order-desk-study.md`) asked what each arm
did with its tools, and one showed that run 1 could not separate a weaker model from a tool
contract that hides errors. Answering them took hand-written scripts and a hand-patched
example. This release puts both into the product, and corrects the public record.

## What ships

- **Tool outcomes in suite reports** (JSON key `tool_outcomes`, a Markdown and an HTML
  section after fault accounting): per arm, trials with a recorded tool failure that
  carries no injection marker, split by whether the trial passed; per tool and arm, trials
  with and without a recorded start. Descriptive only. The record does not show why a call
  failed, and the text says so. No stored record changes; a 2.0.0 store renders with the
  new section.
- **Model swap in the LangChain quickstart**: with two different registered model ids both
  arms keep the same retry, prompt and temperature, and registration labels the arms with
  the model ids. Equal ids and the offline demo keep the retry-removal comparison. In 2.0.0
  a different-model run also removed the candidate's retry; that was a defect of the
  example. Recipe `docs/recipes/model-swap.md`.
- **Public record**: `docs/results/order-desk-study.md` with both pre-registered runs and
  the correction to run 1's first write-up; run 2's report beside run 1's.
- **README**: a "Related tools" section, and the sentence "nothing can PASS before about 80
  paired runs" corrected (79 pairs is the figure when every pair is a tie; pairs in the
  candidate's favour cross sooner).

## The first screen: this record supersedes 0009 for 2.1.0 onward

ADR 0009 fixed the README's text before the first `## ` heading for v0.8.0, at most 200
words, leading with the seeded demo and the `arci init` command for plain Python functions.
0013 kept it. The maintainer approved a change on 2026-11-13: the first screen now leads
with the question in a reader's words, the LangChain quickstart commands and three lines of
their real output, and links to the model-swap and own-agent recipes. Reasons: the one
public post drew readers who use `create_agent`; an external review found no verdict output
before line 80; and the unqualified error-bound sentence needed its assumptions stated.

What stays from 0009: the title, the PASS and INCONCLUSIVE paragraphs and the
"ARCI observes calls" paragraph verbatim; absolute links (PyPI breaks relative ones); no
runtime promise; no comparison table and no adoption claim on the first screen; every
section below the fold in the same order, with "Related tools" added after the 2.0 section.
The `arci init checkout-retry` command remains under "Gate your own agent". The limit
becomes 250 words by `plan/exec/scripts/first_screen_words.py` (the text is 243); one PyPI
badge sits under the title. ADR 0009 is not edited.

## What is not in 2.1.0

Interrupts and human-in-the-loop steps (one reader asked; the adapter still rejects them),
async agents, compound fault conditions, stronger bets near the margin, and every item 0013
listed as not built for suites.

## What the release may and must not say

As in 0013. In addition: the tool-outcome counts are counts of recorded attempts and may
not be described as failures "caused by the agent"; the two study runs are two
observations and may not be described as a measured effect of returning tool errors.
