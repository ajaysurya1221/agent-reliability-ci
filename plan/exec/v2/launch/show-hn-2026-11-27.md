## Text (a draft to rewrite, not to paste)

I work with AI coding agents. I built `realornoise` to answer questions like "Have my Claude
Code edits been failing more often lately?" It compares recent days with earlier days for the
same model and effort setting.

Run `uvx --python 3.14 realornoise` in a terminal. To try generated data without reading your
logs, run `uvx --python 3.14 realornoise --example`. Setup downloads software. The analysis runs
locally and sends nothing.

`REAL DROP` means at least one recorded measure moved in the adverse direction beyond what
shuffling the days would usually explain. `NO DROP` means the data rule out each measure's
preset drop size under the test's assumptions. `CANNOT TELL` is common with short histories.
Work that comes in phases can trigger false alarms. Your tasks, prompts or client may have
changed, and these counts can't tell you whether answers got worse.

## Likely hard questions, with the documents that answer them

| Question | Honest answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Why call fewer tokens worse? | Lower token use is the direction the test flags. Easier tasks can need fewer tokens, and vendors can change how they count them. | `docs/STATISTICS_LOGS.md` |
| How often is the alarm false? | The bound assumes, among other things, that unchanged days can be shuffled. One simulation with work in phases gave 362 false drops in 2,000 histories (18.1%). | `docs/results/logs-selfcheck.md` |
| Why is a week not enough? | Each measure needs ten usable earlier days and five recent ones. Even then, streaks can make the comparison inconclusive. | `docs/recipes/logs.md` |
| Who sees my prompts? | The command reads conversations locally and prints no prompt text. Using the plugin or skill puts its printed report into your assistant conversation. | `docs/PRIVACY.md` |
| Do the laptop studies say how my hosted agent behaves? | No. The order-desk runs tested specific local models, tasks and prompts. They establish nothing about hosted models or your agent. | `docs/results/order-desk-study.md` |
