## What the spike changed in the design

Codex's report shows that when day values are correlated from one day to the next (AR(1),
coefficient 0.5) a wrong REAL DROP appears in 13.7 to 16.7% of runs, with the half-split
self-check in place. Two follow-up simulations by the orchestrator (three independent
measures, log standard deviation 0.45; scripts `orchestrator_guard_rolling.py`, 1,000 runs
per cell, and `orchestrator_guard_runs.py`, 1,500 runs per cell):

| Guard | Wrong REAL DROP at correlation 0.5 (7/21, 14/42, 28/84) | REAL DROP for a 45% drop, independent days (same designs) |
|---|---|---|
| none | 17%, 21%, 20% | 63%, 95%, 100% |
| half-split self-check | 14%, 16%, 15% | 60%, 94%, 98% |
| every placebo window inside the before period, at 1/120 | 10%, 9%, 7% | 55%, 83%, 83% |
| exact runs test on the before-days, at 1/10 | 4%, 1%, 0% | 53%, 75%, 79% |
| every second day, no guard | 4%, 6%, 8% | 28%, 61%, 95% |

The runs test is the best of these. In the contract it replaces the half-split self-check and
is applied per measure (a measure whose earlier days come in streaks is not tested), which
costs less power than a veto by any measure; the final figures are regenerated by
`bench/selfcheck_logs.py` with the final rule.

## Decision

**Go.** Readers, bound and power meet the thresholds of the brief. The contract changes as
above and as in `consults/L1-records-review-codex.md`.
