## Deviations (with reason)
1. In-domain lines keep the dispatch's exact words "under the default rule" even when the manifest's alpha/delta are not the defaults; the numbers are then the manifest's own (alpha and delta are printed in the parentheses). "Default rule" there reads as the gate's fixed-sample Clopper-Pearson rule. Astra should confirm this wording (statistics review is mandatory).
2. Thresholds are searched up to the planner's bound (400), not 10000: one Clopper-Pearson evaluation costs O(N^2) and a full scan to 10000 takes minutes. At or below 400 the scan is exhaustive.
3. `tests/unit/descriptor/test_run_yaml.py` asserted empty stderr for an N=6 `arci run`; it now asserts exactly the notice lines (see report 04).
