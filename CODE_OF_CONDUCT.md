# Code of conduct

Be civil in issues, pull requests and discussions. Disagree with a design or a line of code,
not with the person who wrote it.

Keep discussion on the subject of this repository: bugs, features, the statistics, the
release process. Off-topic promotion or argument may be closed without further notice.

Do not post anyone else's personal data here, including log lines, log file paths, session
content, or anything else that was not already public. `CONTRIBUTING.md` has the rule for
bug reports specifically.

For a private matter, including a suspected vulnerability or a conduct concern you do not
want posted publicly, use the private security advisory link in `SECURITY.md` rather than
a public issue.

This is a single-maintainer project. The maintainer may edit, close or delete a post that
breaks the above, and may block an account that keeps breaking it, without further
explanation.
